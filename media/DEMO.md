# Demo recording

Real Playwright recording of the built-in browser engine: open Pipe stack, draw one sample box, wait for counting, hold the final count (**61**). The pipe image is the app's generated example, not a photograph. No results or UI were substituted.

800 px wide, 12 fps; 2051293 bytes. Recreate with `node scripts/record-demo.cjs` after installing Playwright 1.56.1, Chromium and ffmpeg. The original source video stays in ignored `.demo-recording/`.
