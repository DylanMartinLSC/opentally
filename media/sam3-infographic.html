FSC-147 (Ranjan et al., CVPR 2021): 578 photos in 11 everyday categories, from pills and coins to bricks and logs. MixCount (Dumery et al., CC BY 4.0) for look-alike kinds. One sample box per photo, confidence 0.6, RTX 3090. Full method in the repo.
